Site photo reports, built on your phone

Photo Report Build helps construction teams document work on site. Take a photo, mark where you are on the plan, answer the checklist and send a clean PDF report.

Published by Alejandro Sebastian Perez, trading as SebastianBuildTech, Ireland.

What it does

  • Stamped photos. Project, area, service, item name and ITM location are added to every photo.
  • Plan position. Mark where each photo was taken on the plan of the area.
  • Checklists. Answer Yes, No or N/A to the questions set up by your team.
  • PDF reports. A cover page, your logos and four photos per page, ready to share.

Your data stays with you

The app has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Photos, locations and reports are stored on your phone. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.
