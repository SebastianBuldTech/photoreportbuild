Privacy Policy

Photo Report Build · Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: Photo Report Build works on your phone. It has no user accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. The photos, locations and reports you create stay on your device unless you choose to share them. We do not receive them and cannot see them.

1. Who we are

Photo Report Build (the “app”) is published by Alejandro Sebastian Perez, trading as SebastianBuildTech, Ireland (“we”, “us”).

Contact: ing.alejandro.sebastian@gmail.com

2. What the app handles on your device

To do its job, the app creates and stores the following information on your device:

  • Photos you take with the app, with optional text and logo stamped on the image.
  • Location. When you take a photo, the app reads your device’s precise location (GPS) and converts it to Irish Transverse Mercator (ITM) coordinates. The coordinates are saved with the photo, can be stamped on it, and appear in the PDF reports you create. The app reads your location only while the camera screen is open.
  • Report content you type or select: name of the item photographed, project, area and service, the person who made the report, answers to checklist questions, notes, and the point you mark on a plan.
  • Configuration file you import (a ZIP file prepared by you or your organisation) with lists of projects, areas, names, questions, logos and plans.
  • PDF reports you generate.
  • Settings, such as the defaults you choose.

3. Where it is stored

Everything above is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. We do not operate any server that receives it.

When you save a report, the app also saves a copy of each photo in your device’s gallery, in an album called PhotoReport. Other apps that have permission to access your photos can see those copies. Deleting a report in the app, or uninstalling the app, does not delete the copies in your gallery; you can delete them yourself from your gallery or file manager.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos.
  • Location: to record where each photo was taken.
  • Storage (Android 9 and earlier only): to save photo copies in the gallery.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings. The app will then be unable to offer the matching feature.

5. Sharing

The app does not send your data anywhere on its own. If you choose to share a PDF or a photo (for example by email or a messaging app), you do so through your device’s share menu, and the app you choose handles the data under its own privacy policy.

6. Third-party services

The app has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services. On Android, your device’s location may be provided through Google Play services, which is governed by Google’s own privacy policy. If you install the app from Google Play, Google may collect information about the installation under its own terms.

7. Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete reports inside the app, which removes their photos from the app’s storage. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored inside it. Copies saved in your gallery and PDF files you have shared must be deleted separately.

9. Security

The app’s data is kept in storage that Android protects from other apps. The app does not add its own encryption, so we recommend that you protect your device with a screen lock and keep it up to date.

10. If you use the app for work

Your reports may include personal data of other people, for example names of colleagues or people who appear in photos. If you use the app for your work, you or your organisation decide what is recorded and are responsible for having a lawful basis, informing the people concerned and following your own data protection duties. Where possible, avoid photographing people.

11. Your rights

Under data protection law (including the GDPR), you have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction and deletion. Because we do not collect or hold your data, we have nothing to access or delete on our side; your data is under your control on your device. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at the address above. You also have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (www.dataprotection.ie).

12. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the app.
